What is Modelica?

Modelica is an equation-based, multi-domain language for modeling physical and cyber-physical systems (mechanical, electrical, thermal, hydraulics, control logic and more).

Learn more at Modelica.org Try Wolfram System Modeler (trial)

About the author

Ankit Anurag Naik is an Application Engineer at Wolfram MathCore AB, working on multidisciplinary system modeling, simulation, and workflows that connect modeling to analysis, optimization, and communication. He writes practical books that make Modelica accessible to engineers at every level.
